Project 6: Simple Cars

At a glance

Goal: Design and build a simple car using as few parts as possible.

The Simple Car

I designed and built a simple car using as few parts as possible. This simple car only consisted of Spike Prime Hub, 2 motors, 2 wheels, 2 joint-socket like connectors, and 2 wire management parts. I programmed it to move forward and backward at slow and fast speeds, rotate in place clockwise and counterclockwise, and move in small and large arcs. (see Demo Video 1)
